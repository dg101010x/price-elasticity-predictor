How this works, and what it isn't

Every estimate here comes from 1.07 million real transactions at a UK online gift and homeware wholesaler between December 2009 and December 2011 (UCI Online Retail II). Sales are rolled up to one row per product per week, and each product is compared against its own history — so the answer reflects what happened when that product's price moved, not the fact that expensive things sell in smaller numbers.

The three things this can't tell you

  • It's a pattern, not a promise. Nobody ran a pricing experiment here. Prices moved for reasons — promotions, seasons, clearance — and those reasons moved sales too. Read this as evidence, not a forecast.
  • Revenue is not profit. A discount that grows revenue can still shrink what you keep. Add your unit cost in the panel on the left to see both.
  • One catalogue, one market. These are wholesale gift and homeware prices in GBP. The direction of the effect usually travels; the exact numbers don't.
