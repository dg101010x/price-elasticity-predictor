What each of these numbers does and doesn’t mean

Where a fitted number disagrees with the published literature, or where the dataset can’t quite answer the question asked of it, it says so here rather than somewhere less visible.

Brand switching is a different, much bigger number

These come from supermarket panels that record which brand a shopper picked, not how many units the category sold. Raising one brand's price sends shoppers to the brand beside it, which is a far larger effect than the whole category buying less — so these numbers belong to a different question and are kept off the chart above.
